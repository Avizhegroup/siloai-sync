# Silo System Overview

## Introduction

Silo is a warehouse management application developed by Tadbir Dade Avizhe.

The system is designed to help organizations manage, identify, and track products and other unique entities throughout their operational lifecycle.

Silo uses automated identification technologies, including RFID technology based on UHF frequencies, to improve the speed, accuracy, and reliability of warehouse and operational processes.

---

## Warehouse Management

Silo helps warehouse managers and operators manage different aspects of warehouse operations.

The system supports capabilities such as:

- مدیریت موجودی انبار
- ردیابی محصولات با تکنولوژی RFID
- تولید گزارش‌های مختلف
- بهینه‌سازی سرعت و دقت عملیات انبارداری
- مدیریت ورود و خروج کالا
- کنترل زون‌های مختلف انبار

The system is designed to improve the visibility, accuracy, and efficiency of warehouse operations.

---

## Product and Entity Identification

Silo can manage unique entities by assigning unique serial numbers to them.

This allows individual products, packages, or other trackable entities to be identified and distinguished from one another.

A unique identifier can be used to maintain information and historical records related to each managed entity.

---

## RFID and Automated Identification

Silo supports automated identification technologies to simplify operational processes.

Depending on the project and system configuration, identification can be performed using:

- UHF RFID technology
- RFID gates
- Handheld RFID devices
- Barcode scanners

These technologies help automate the identification and processing of products and other trackable entities.

Using automated identification can improve the speed and accuracy of operational and warehouse processes.

---

## Product Tracking

The system supports the tracking of products and other managed entities throughout different stages of their operational lifecycle.

By using unique identification methods such as serial numbers and RFID tags, the system can help maintain traceability and historical information related to tracked entities.

This can provide better visibility over products and warehouse operations.

---

## Warehouse Zones

Silo can be used to manage and control different warehouse zones.

Zone-related information can be used as part of the warehouse management and tracking process.

---

## Reports and Operational Analysis

Silo provides features for generating reports based on available system data.

Reports can help users review, monitor, and analyze warehouse and operational information.

The available reports and their capabilities depend on the documented features of the system.

---

## Flexibility and Customization

Silo is designed to support different industries and operational requirements.

The terminology, information fields, workflows, and operational requirements may vary depending on the project configuration.

This flexibility allows Silo to support different types of products, industries, and warehouse environments.

---

## Summary

Silo is a warehouse management and tracking application designed to improve the management, identification, traceability, and monitoring of products and warehouse operations.

The main capabilities include:

- مدیریت موجودی انبار
- ردیابی محصولات با استفاده از RFID
- مدیریت ورود و خروج کالا
- کنترل زون‌های مختلف انبار
- شناسایی محصولات با استفاده از RFID و بارکد
- تولید گزارش و تحلیل اطلاعات عملیاتی
- افزایش سرعت و دقت عملیات انبارداری
- نگهداری اطلاعات و سوابق مرتبط با کالاهای قابل ردیابی
